Stock Price Prediction Using Deep Learning:
LSTM and GRU Models

A Deep Learning-Based Time Series Analysis of ADANIPORTS stock prices. Implements an end-to-end mathematical and computational pipeline featuring isolated data scaling, chronological 60-day sliding-window sequences, gated recurrent neural networks, and rigorous regression diagnostics.

Open Analytics Dashboard Next-Day Close Forecast Model Comparison (LSTM vs GRU)

End-to-End System Pipeline

A zero-data-leakage architecture strictly adhering to financial econometrics & deep learning standards.

1

Dataset Ingestion

Historical daily quotes of ADANIPORTS from 2007 to 2021 (3,322 trading records) with automatic schema detection.

2

Time-Series Integrity

Strict chronological sorting, duplicate date removal, missing value imputation (ffill/bfill), and price validity checks.

3

Technical Features

Constructs 20, 50, & 100-day Simple Moving Averages, daily percentage returns, and rolling volatility.

4

Sequence Generator

Generates sliding windows of 60 trading days ($t-59$ to $t$) to forecast the next trading day's Close ($t+1$).

5

Deep Learning

Trains custom 2-layer stacked LSTM and GRU networks with Dropout (0.2), Adam optimization, and EarlyStopping.

6

Evaluation & Serving

Computes MAE, MSE, RMSE, MAPE, and R² on unscaled INR prices, selecting the best model for live Flask inference.

Dataset Characteristics

Asset Ticker ADANIPORTS (Formerly MUNDRAPORT)
Trading Period 2007-11-27 to 2021-04-30 (13.5 Years)
Total Trading Days 3,322 Sessions
Target Variable $Close(t+1)$ (Next Trading Day Close)
Window / Sequence Size 60 Trading Days
Train / Test Split 80% Chronological Train / 20% Test (665 Days)
