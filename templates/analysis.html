Financial Econometric Insights

  • Volatility Clustering: Large price variations tend to be followed by large variations, visible in the rolling volatility chart.

  • Non-Stationarity: Historical prices exhibit strong non-stationarity and upward drift, justifying MinMaxScaler fitting strictly on training intervals.
